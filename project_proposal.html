YOUTH DIGITAL AND GREEN INNOVATION EMPOWERMENT INITIATIVE

PROJECT PROPOSAL

Applicant Organization: Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub (GADEH CBO)

Funding Requested: KES 5,000,000

Project Duration: 24 Months

Project Location: Garissa County, Kenya

Target Beneficiaries: Youth aged approximately 18–35 years

Focus Areas: Digital Skills, Technology, Entrepreneurship, Innovation and Green Solutions

1. Executive Summary

Garissa County has a large and growing population of young people, yet many youth face limited access to digital skills, affordable internet connectivity, employment opportunities, entrepreneurship support and practical pathways into the digital economy.

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub (GADEH CBO) proposes the Youth Digital and Green Innovation Empowerment Initiative (YDGEI), a 24-month project designed to equip young people with practical digital, entrepreneurial and green innovation skills while creating opportunities for employment, self-employment and community-based innovation.

The project will establish and strengthen an accessible youth digital empowerment hub where young people can receive training in digital literacy, computer applications, graphic design, digital marketing, web development, online work, entrepreneurship and other relevant digital skills.

The initiative will also introduce green technology and environmental awareness components, including digital solutions for environmental challenges, responsible electronic waste management and youth-led green enterprises.

The project aims to directly reach approximately 1,000 young people, with particular attention to unemployed and underemployed youth, young women, youth with disabilities and young people from underserved communities.

3. Problem Statement

Youth unemployment and limited economic opportunities remain significant challenges in many parts of Garissa County. Although young people increasingly have access to mobile phones and digital platforms, many lack the practical skills, equipment, connectivity and mentorship required to convert technology into sustainable economic opportunities.

Young people in underserved communities also face barriers including:

  • Limited access to computers and reliable internet.
  • Inadequate practical digital skills.
  • Limited knowledge of online employment and freelancing.
  • Lack of entrepreneurship and business development skills.
  • Limited access to mentors and professional networks.
  • Low exposure to technology-based innovation.
  • Limited participation of young women in technology.
  • Inadequate awareness of environmentally sustainable technologies and e-waste management.

Without targeted interventions, many young people risk remaining excluded from the rapidly expanding digital economy.

The proposed project responds to these challenges by combining digital empowerment, entrepreneurship, innovation and green solutions within one youth-focused initiative.

4. Overall Goal

To increase economic opportunities and community resilience among young people in Garissa County through access to digital skills, technology, entrepreneurship and green innovation.

5. Specific Objectives

  1. To provide practical digital skills training to at least 1,000 young people over 24 months.
  2. To support at least 200 youth to develop income-generating activities, digital enterprises, freelancing careers or technology-based businesses.
  3. To establish a youth innovation environment where young people can develop technology-based and environmentally sustainable solutions to community challenges.
  4. To improve access to computers, internet connectivity, digital learning resources and mentorship for underserved youth.
  5. To strengthen youth participation in green innovation, responsible e-waste management and environmentally sustainable entrepreneurship.

6. Target Beneficiaries

The primary beneficiaries will be young people aged approximately 18–35 years in Garissa County.

Priority will be given to:

  • Unemployed and underemployed youth.
  • Young women.
  • Youth from low-income households.
  • Youth with disabilities.
  • Youth without access to computers or reliable internet.
  • Young entrepreneurs and aspiring entrepreneurs.
  • Youth interested in technology and innovation.

The project will also indirectly benefit families, community organizations, local businesses and other community institutions that benefit from youth-led digital services and innovations.

7. Key Project Activities

A. Digital Skills Training

The project will provide practical training in:

  • Basic computer literacy.
  • Microsoft Office and productivity tools.
  • Internet and digital literacy.
  • Graphic design.
  • Digital marketing.
  • Website development.
  • Online communication and collaboration.
  • Digital entrepreneurship.
  • Freelancing and remote work.
  • Online business development.
  • Cybersecurity and responsible digital citizenship.

B. Youth Entrepreneurship Development

Participants will receive training and mentorship on:

  • Business idea development.
  • Business planning.
  • Financial literacy.
  • Digital marketing.
  • Customer acquisition.
  • Record keeping.
  • Business registration and compliance.
  • Using digital platforms to access markets.

C. Youth Innovation Programme

The project will organize innovation challenges where youth teams identify local problems and develop technology-based solutions.

Potential areas include:

  • Agriculture.
  • Education.
  • Healthcare information.
  • Digital commerce.
  • Water management.
  • Waste management.
  • Climate resilience.
  • Community information systems.

D. Green Technology and Environmental Action

  • E-waste awareness.
  • Responsible disposal and recycling of electronic equipment.
  • Training on energy-efficient technology.
  • Youth-led environmental campaigns.
  • Development of green digital businesses.
  • Promotion of technology solutions addressing environmental challenges.

E. Digital Access Hub

The project will strengthen the organization's digital hub with:

  • Computers.
  • Internet connectivity.
  • Digital learning resources.
  • Training equipment.
  • Appropriate networking equipment.
  • Power backup solutions.

F. Mentorship and Career Linkages

Participants will be connected with technology professionals, entrepreneurs, trainers, employers, freelancing platforms, business development organizations and relevant government and private-sector institutions.

8. Expected Outputs

  1. At least 1,000 youth will receive digital and entrepreneurship training.
  2. At least 200 youth will be supported toward income-generating opportunities.
  3. At least 50 youth-led digital/green ideas will be developed.
  4. At least 20 promising innovations or enterprises will receive intensive mentorship.
  5. A functional youth digital empowerment hub will be strengthened.
  6. Youth will gain improved access to computers and internet connectivity.
  7. Youth will receive practical exposure to green technology and e-waste management.
  8. Partnerships will be developed with relevant stakeholders.

9. Expected Outcomes

  • Increased digital literacy among youth.
  • Increased youth employability.
  • Increased youth entrepreneurship.
  • Increased access to online work opportunities.
  • Increased participation of young women in digital opportunities.
  • Increased youth-led innovation.
  • Improved awareness of environmental sustainability.
  • Increased use of technology to address local challenges.

10. Sustainability

The project will establish mechanisms that allow the digital hub to continue supporting young people beyond the grant period.

  • Affordable digital services at the hub.
  • Partnerships with local businesses and institutions.
  • Youth membership programmes.
  • Entrepreneurship and innovation partnerships.
  • Training-of-trainers approaches.
  • Volunteer mentors.
  • Revenue-generating digital services.
  • Partnerships with technology companies and development organizations.

A portion of the hub's future income will be reinvested into equipment maintenance, internet connectivity and youth training.

11. Monitoring and Evaluation

The project will establish a monitoring and evaluation framework to measure progress and demonstrate results.

Key indicators will include:

  • Number of youth enrolled.
  • Number completing training.
  • Number of women participating.
  • Number of youth receiving mentorship.
  • Number of youth starting businesses.
  • Number accessing online employment.
  • Number of innovations developed.
  • Number of youth-led green initiatives.
  • Number of partnerships established.
  • Income opportunities created.

Baseline information will be collected at the beginning of the project, followed by periodic monitoring, participant feedback and an end-of-project evaluation.

14. Conclusion

The Youth Digital and Green Innovation Empowerment Initiative presents an opportunity to transform the potential of young people in Garissa County into practical economic and community solutions.

By combining digital skills, technology access, entrepreneurship, innovation and environmental sustainability, the project will create pathways for young people to acquire skills, develop businesses, access employment and contribute to solving local challenges.

GDEH CBO seeks support of KES 5,000,000 to implement this 24-month initiative and establish a sustainable model of youth digital and green empowerment that can be expanded to additional communities in the future.
