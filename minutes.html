7 Adoption of the Constitution

7.1 Presentation of the Constitution

The members considered the proposed Constitution as the principal governing document establishing the organization's objectives, membership structure, leadership responsibilities, meetings, financial administration, accountability mechanisms and other governance matters.

The Constitution was presented to the members for consideration, discussion and formal adoption.

7.2 Members' Consideration

Members were given an opportunity to review and discuss the provisions of the Constitution. The members recognized the importance of having a formal governing document to guide the organization's operations and promote transparency, accountability, responsible leadership and proper administration.

7.3 Adoption Resolution

Constitutional Resolution

IT WAS RESOLVED that the Constitution of the organization be and is hereby adopted as the governing constitutional document of the organization.

The members agreed to be guided by the adopted Constitution in the management, governance, administration and implementation of organizational activities.

The adopted Constitution shall be maintained as an official organizational record and shall serve as a reference for future meetings, decisions and governance processes.

7.4 Custody of the Constitutional Record

The Secretary was tasked with maintaining the official copy of the adopted Constitution together with the organization's minutes, attendance records and other governance documents.

8 Official Certificate of Registration

8.1 Registration Documentation

The meeting discussed the importance of obtaining the official Certificate of Registration for the organization through the appropriate Government of Kenya registration process.

Members specifically discussed pursuing the registration process and obtaining the official certificate from the Ministry of Labour, Gender and Social Protection or the applicable registration authority responsible for Community-Based Organizations.

Members recognized that official registration documentation is important in establishing the organization's formal status and supporting legitimate operations, partnerships, programmes, financial administration and accountability.

8.2 Registration Resolution

Registration Resolution

IT WAS RESOLVED that the organization shall proceed with the necessary administrative steps to obtain its official Certificate of Registration through the appropriate Government of Kenya registration process.

The organization shall prepare and submit the Constitution, minutes, membership information, identification documents, application forms and any other documents required by the relevant registration authority.

The leadership shall make reasonable efforts to ensure that the registration process is completed properly and that the official certificate is securely maintained as part of the organization's permanent records.

8.3 Responsibility for Registration

The Chairperson and Secretary shall coordinate the preparation and submission of registration documents, with support from the Treasurer and other members where necessary.

9 Opening of Organizational Bank Account

9.1 Need for an Official Bank Account

The meeting discussed the importance of establishing an official bank account to provide a secure and accountable mechanism for receiving, holding and disbursing organizational funds.

Members agreed that organizational finances should be separated from personal finances and managed through an official account held in the organization's name, subject to applicable banking requirements.

9.2 Proposed Bank

Members considered Equity Bank, Garissa Branch as the proposed banking institution for the organization's official bank account.

9.3 Banking Resolution

Bank Account Resolution

IT WAS RESOLVED that the organization shall proceed with the opening of an official organizational bank account with Equity Bank, Garissa Branch, subject to the bank's account-opening requirements and applicable laws and regulations.

The account shall be used for legitimate organizational transactions, including receipt of grants, donations, programme funds, member contributions and other lawful organizational income, together with payment of approved organizational expenses.

All transactions shall be undertaken in accordance with the organization's Constitution, approved financial procedures, internal controls and applicable banking requirements.

9.4 Required Documentation

The organization shall provide the required registration documents, Constitution, meeting minutes, identification documents, leadership information, registration certificate when available and any other documents requested by the bank.

9.5 Financial Accountability

The Treasurer shall support the maintenance of accurate financial records. Organizational funds shall be used only for legitimate purposes consistent with the organization's objectives and properly authorized activities.

10 Organizational Objectives and Strategic Direction

The meeting discussed the importance of establishing the organization around practical community development initiatives, with particular attention to agriculture, digital empowerment, agro-entrepreneurship, youth participation and sustainable livelihoods.

Members recognized the importance of promoting practical approaches that can strengthen agricultural knowledge, encourage innovation, improve access to digital opportunities and contribute to resilient community livelihoods.

Strategic Areas of Focus
  • Agro-entrepreneurship and agricultural enterprise development.
  • Climate-smart, sustainable and water-efficient agriculture.
  • Digital literacy and computer skills development, including computer packages and basic office applications.
  • Information and Communication Technology (ICT) training, digital skills and technology awareness.
  • Graphic design, digital content creation and creative technology skills.
  • Computer repair, maintenance, troubleshooting and basic technical support.
  • Help desk systems, digital customer support and basic information-management skills.
  • Smartphone literacy, mobile technology and responsible use of digital services.
  • ICT short courses, practical skills training, mentorship and digital entrepreneurship.
  • Digital agriculture, agricultural information systems and technology-enabled farmer services.
  • Farmer education, agricultural training and knowledge sharing.
  • Youth innovation, entrepreneurship and meaningful participation in community development.
  • Women empowerment, skills development and livelihood opportunities.
  • Environmental conservation, tree planting, ecosystem restoration and sustainable natural-resource management.
  • Community empowerment, livelihood development and socio-economic inclusion.
  • Partnerships, networking, resource mobilization and collaboration with relevant stakeholders.

11 Membership Responsibilities

Members acknowledged the importance of active participation in the organization and agreed that members should support its objectives, values and lawful activities.

  • Attend and participate in official meetings and organizational activities where reasonably possible.
  • Respect decisions properly adopted through the organization's governance structures.
  • Protect organizational property, documents and resources.
  • Promote integrity, transparency and accountability.
  • Respect fellow members, beneficiaries and community stakeholders.
  • Support programmes and community development initiatives.
  • Maintain confidentiality where organizational information is properly designated as confidential.
  • Avoid conduct that may damage the legitimate interests or reputation of the organization.

12 Governance and Administration

The meeting emphasized the importance of transparent, accountable and properly documented organizational administration.

The organization shall maintain appropriate governance records and conduct its activities in accordance with its Constitution, approved policies, decisions of members and applicable laws of the Republic of Kenya.

Governance Principle

Organizational decisions, records, finances, property and resources should be handled with integrity, accountability, transparency and responsibility.

13 Programmes and Community Activities

Members discussed the importance of developing practical programmes that respond to community needs and contribute to agricultural, digital, entrepreneurial and socio-economic development.

Potential activities may include farmer training, agro-entrepreneurship development, digital skills training, agricultural demonstrations, youth innovation, environmental conservation, community awareness and other initiatives consistent with the organization's objectives.

Members agreed that future programmes should be planned, documented and implemented responsibly, with attention to community participation, sustainability and measurable outcomes.

14 Financial Accountability

The meeting recognized that responsible financial management is essential to the credibility, sustainability and proper administration of the organization.

All organizational funds and resources shall be managed responsibly and used only for legitimate purposes consistent with the organization's objectives and approved activities.

  • Promote transparency in financial decisions.
  • Safeguard organizational funds and property.
  • Maintain supporting documentation for financial transactions.

  • Promote accountability to members and relevant stakeholders.
