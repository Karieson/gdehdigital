GDEH Workshops

Digital Skills • Agro-Entrepreneurship • Innovation • Community Empowerment

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO (GDEH CBO) provides practical community empowerment workshops designed to equip youth, women, farmers, entrepreneurs and other community members with relevant skills for today's digital and agricultural economy.

Our workshops combine digital literacy, entrepreneurship, agriculture and innovation to help participants improve their employability, start or strengthen enterprises, access online opportunities and adopt modern approaches to agriculture.

Training activities are conducted in a practical, accessible and supportive learning environment, with emphasis on skills that participants can apply in their daily lives, businesses and communities.

Training Workshops Include

  • Computer & Digital Literacy
  • Smartphone & Mobile Technology Skills
  • Agro-Entrepreneurship
  • Digital Agriculture
  • Climate-Smart Agriculture
  • Agricultural Business & Market Access
  • Cybersecurity & Online Safety
  • Online Platforms & Digital Services
  • Entrepreneurship & Business Skills
  • Digital Marketing & Online Business

Practical Learning

Participants are guided through practical exercises, demonstrations, discussions and hands-on activities. Training is designed to be easy to understand and accessible to participants with different levels of previous experience.

Our trainers and facilitators encourage participants to apply what they learn to real-life challenges, businesses, agricultural activities and community development initiatives.

What Participants Can Gain

Digital Skills: Build confidence in using computers, smartphones, internet services and digital platforms.

Entrepreneurship: Develop practical knowledge for starting and managing small enterprises.

Agro-Entrepreneurship: Learn how agriculture can be developed into sustainable and profitable business opportunities.

Digital Agriculture: Understand how technology can support agricultural production, information access, marketing and decision-making.

Online Safety: Develop awareness of cybersecurity, privacy, scams and responsible use of online platforms.

Workshop Contribution & Certificate

Our workshops are designed as affordable community empowerment programmes.

A small management and maintenance contribution of KES 1,000 may apply to selected workshops. This contribution supports training resources, equipment maintenance, internet access, learning materials and smooth workshop operations.

Participants who successfully complete the required workshop sessions may receive a Certificate of Participation, subject to the requirements of the specific programme.

Who Can Participate?

Our workshops are open to youth, women, farmers, entrepreneurs, students, persons with disabilities and other community members interested in developing practical digital, agricultural and entrepreneurial skills.
