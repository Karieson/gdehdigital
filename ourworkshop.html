GDEH Workshops

Our Community Based Organization (CBO) operates in Garissa Township, Kenya, offering free empowerment skills and professional workshops every weekday throughout the calendar year. The workshops are designed to equip youth, women, and community members with practical modern skills that create opportunities for employment, self-development, entrepreneurship, and digital empowerment.

Training Workshops Include

  • Computer Literacy
  • Smartphone Literacy
  • Agro Entrepreneurship
  • Cyber Security
  • Online Platforms Training

Participants are guided through practical and easy-to-understand sessions by experienced trainers in a friendly learning environment. Our goal is to bridge the digital skills gap and empower communities with knowledge that can improve livelihoods and open new opportunities.

Although the workshops are community empowerment programs, a small management and maintenance contribution fee of only 1000 KSH is applicable. This helps support training resources, equipment maintenance, internet access, and smooth workshop operations for all participants.

A Certificate of Participation is also available for attendees who successfully complete the workshop sessions.
