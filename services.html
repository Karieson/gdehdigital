Digital Literacy Training

We provide comprehensive computer packages and ICT short courses covering Microsoft Office, internet applications, web design, graphic design, and cybersecurity basics. These trainings equip learners with essential digital skills for education, employment, and entrepreneurship.

Smartphone Literacy

Our smartphone literacy programs help participants understand Android basics, internet browsing, social media use, and mobile money applications. This service empowers community members to use smartphones effectively for communication, business, and financial inclusion.

Agro-Entrepreneurship

We train youth and farmers in agribusiness management, crop production techniques, farm record keeping, and agricultural marketing. This service promotes sustainable farming practices and entrepreneurship opportunities in agriculture.

Workshops & Mentorship

GDEH CBO organizes workshops and mentorship sessions to support innovation, creativity, and career growth. These sessions provide hands-on learning, guidance from experts, and opportunities to network with peers and professionals.

Community Empowerment Programs

We run inclusive programs targeting women, persons with disabilities, and vulnerable groups. These services ensure equal access to digital tools, entrepreneurship training, and community development opportunities.

Youth Employment & Innovation Hub

Our hub serves as a platform for youth to access resources, mentorship, and training that enhance employability and self-employment. We encourage innovation and creativity, helping young people turn ideas into sustainable projects.
