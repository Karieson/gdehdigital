Welcome To GADEH CBO

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub is a community-based organization committed to empowering youth, women, persons with disabilities and underserved communities through digital literacy, technology innovation and agro-entrepreneurship.

We provide practical skills, knowledge and opportunities that enable communities to embrace technology, develop sustainable enterprises, improve livelihoods and participate meaningfully in the digital economy.
