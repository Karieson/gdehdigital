WELCOME TO GADEH CBO

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub (GADEH) is a community-based organization committed to empowering youth, women, persons with disabilities and underserved communities through digital literacy, technology innovation and agro-entrepreneurship.

We believe that access to digital knowledge, practical skills and innovative opportunities can transform communities. Our work focuses on bridging the digital divide and ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to participate in the rapidly evolving digital economy.

Through digital training, technology awareness and hands-on learning, we support individuals in developing the confidence and competencies needed to use digital tools, access information and explore emerging economic opportunities.

We also promote agro-digital innovation by connecting agriculture with technology, entrepreneurship and sustainable practices. We encourage communities to embrace modern agricultural solutions, improve productivity, strengthen market access and build resilient livelihoods.

Our approach is rooted in inclusion, community participation, collaboration and locally driven solutions. We work towards creating opportunities for marginalized groups while encouraging partnerships that contribute to sustainable development and social empowerment.

At GADEH, we envision empowered communities where digital innovation and sustainable agriculture work together to create opportunities, improve livelihoods and build a more inclusive and prosperous future.
