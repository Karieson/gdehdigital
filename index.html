Welcome To GDEH CBO

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub (GDEH CBO) empowers youth, women, PWDs and communities through digital innovation, entrepreneurship, technology access and modern skills development.

We provide practical digital training, ICT workshops, mentorship and empowerment programmes designed to create opportunities for the next generation.

Are you a youth, person living with disability (PWD), entrepreneur or someone passionate about digital empowerment and innovation?

Admissions are now open for our digital training, ICT workshops and empowerment programmes. Join our growing innovation community and gain modern digital skills for the future.
