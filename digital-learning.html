About the learning centre

Agricultural Knowledge for Every Farmer

The GADEH Digital Agricultural Learning Centre provides accessible information to farmers, agricultural learners and community members.

The learning centre covers crop production, soil management, irrigation, crop health, climate-smart agriculture, harvesting and agricultural marketing.

Our goal is to make useful agricultural knowledge easier to access and apply in everyday farming.
