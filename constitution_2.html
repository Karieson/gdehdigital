CONSTITUTION

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO

GADEH CBO

P PREAMBLE

We, the members of Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO, recognizing the importance of agriculture, digital technology, entrepreneurship, environmental conservation, youth empowerment and community participation in improving livelihoods, hereby establish this Constitution as the governing framework of the Organization.

We are committed to promoting inclusive, sustainable and community-driven development by empowering farmers, youth, women, entrepreneurs and other community members through agricultural knowledge, digital innovation, skills development, enterprise development, environmental stewardship and access to opportunities.

We therefore adopt this Constitution to guide the governance, management, operations, accountability and development of Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO.

1 NAME AND IDENTITY

1.1 Name

The name of the organization shall be Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO , hereinafter referred to as "the Organization" or "GADEH CBO".

1.2 Acronym

The official acronym of the Organization shall be GADEH CBO.

1.3 Nature of the Organization

GADEH CBO shall be a community-based, non-profit, non-partisan organization established to promote agricultural development, digital empowerment, entrepreneurship, environmental conservation, youth participation and sustainable community development.

1.4 Non-Profit Character

The income and property of the Organization shall be applied solely toward achieving its objectives. No portion shall be distributed directly or indirectly among members except for legitimate reimbursement or approved payments for services rendered to the Organization.

2 REGISTERED OFFICE

The principal office of the Organization shall be situated within Garissa County, Kenya, at an address approved by the Executive Committee and recorded in the official organizational records.

3 VISION, MISSION AND VALUES

3.1 Vision

To build empowered, digitally connected and economically resilient communities through sustainable agriculture, innovation and inclusive development.

3.2 Mission

To empower farmers, youth, women and communities through agricultural skills, digital technologies, entrepreneurship, environmental conservation, access to information and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

3.3 Core Values

  • Integrity and honesty
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Community participation
  • Innovation and creativity
  • Environmental responsibility
  • Equality and inclusion
  • Professionalism
  • Teamwork
  • Respect and dignity
  • Sustainability

4 OBJECTIVES

The objectives of GADEH CBO shall include:

  1. To promote sustainable and climate-smart agriculture.
  2. To empower farmers with modern agricultural knowledge, technologies and practices.
  3. To promote digital literacy and digital agricultural advisory services.
  4. To support agricultural entrepreneurship and community enterprises.
  5. To promote youth participation in agriculture, technology and innovation.
  6. To support women and vulnerable community members in accessing skills, opportunities and economic activities.
  7. To promote water-efficient farming and responsible natural-resource management.
  8. To promote environmental conservation, tree planting, land restoration and climate resilience.
  9. To organize training, workshops, demonstrations, mentorship and community awareness programmes.
  10. To promote access to markets, finance, information, technology and agricultural services.
  11. To encourage research, innovation and community-based solutions.
  12. To establish partnerships with government institutions, private organizations, NGOs, development partners, educational institutions and other stakeholders.
  13. To undertake lawful income-generating activities that support the sustainability of the Organization and its community programmes.

5 MEMBERSHIP

5.1 Eligibility

Membership shall be open to persons who support the objectives and values of GADEH CBO and are willing to comply with this Constitution and applicable laws.

5.2 Categories of Membership

  • Founder Members
  • Ordinary Members
  • Youth Members
  • Associate Members
  • Honorary Members

5.3 Admission

Applications for membership shall be submitted through the approved membership process and considered in accordance with organizational procedures.

5.4 Membership Register

The Organization shall maintain an up-to-date membership register containing relevant membership information.

5.5 Termination of Membership

Membership may cease through resignation, death, expulsion following due process, or other lawful grounds provided under this Constitution.

6 RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF MEMBERS

6.1 Rights

  • To participate in lawful activities of the Organization.
  • To attend General Membership Meetings.
  • To vote where eligible.
  • To seek elective office where eligible.
  • To receive appropriate information about organizational activities.
  • To raise concerns and make recommendations.

6.2 Responsibilities

  • To comply with this Constitution.
  • To uphold the reputation of GADEH CBO.
  • To participate in organizational activities.
  • To protect organizational property.
  • To promote transparency and accountability.
  • To respect other members and beneficiaries.

7 GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

The governance structure of GADEH CBO shall consist of:

  1. General Membership Meeting
  2. Executive Committee
  3. Project and Technical Committees
  4. Secretariat or staff, where applicable

The General Membership shall remain the supreme decision-making body of the Organization.

8 GENERAL MEMBERSHIP MEETING

8.1 Authority

The General Membership Meeting shall have authority to determine the broad direction, policies and priorities of the Organization.

8.2 Annual General Meeting

The Organization shall hold an Annual General Meeting once every calendar year.

8.3 Special General Meeting

A Special General Meeting may be called by the Chairperson, Executive Committee or through a written request by eligible members in accordance with organizational procedures.

8.4 Quorum

Unless otherwise required by applicable law, the quorum for a General Membership Meeting shall be at least one-half of eligible voting members.

8.5 Decision Making

Decisions shall preferably be reached through consensus. Where consensus cannot be achieved, decisions may be made through a simple majority vote unless this Constitution provides otherwise.

9 EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

The Executive Committee shall be responsible for overseeing the affairs and implementation of decisions of the General Membership.

10 DUTIES OF OFFICE BEARERS

10.1 Chairperson

  • Provide strategic leadership.
  • Chair organizational meetings.
  • Represent the Organization where authorized.
  • Promote accountability and unity.

10.2 Vice Chairperson

  • Assist the Chairperson.
  • Perform the duties of the Chairperson when required.

10.3 Secretary

  • Maintain official records.
  • Prepare meeting notices and agendas.
  • Record and maintain minutes.
  • Manage official correspondence.

10.4 Treasurer

  • Maintain financial records.
  • Prepare financial reports.
  • Safeguard financial documents.
  • Monitor income and expenditure.

10.5 Programme/Organizing Secretary

  • Coordinate community activities.
  • Support programme implementation.
  • Mobilize members and beneficiaries.

11 ELECTIONS AND TENURE

Office bearers shall be elected by eligible members through a transparent and democratic process.

The term of office shall be determined by the General Membership and shall comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

An office bearer may be removed for misconduct, abuse of office, persistent failure to perform duties, financial impropriety or serious violation of this Constitution, provided that due process and an opportunity to be heard are provided.

12 FINANCE AND PROPERTY

12.1 Sources of Funds

  • Membership contributions and approved fees.
  • Donations and grants.
  • Partnership contributions.
  • Fundraising activities.
  • Approved income-generating activities.
  • Other lawful sources.

12.2 Bank Account

The Organization shall maintain an account with a regulated financial institution approved in accordance with its governance and applicable requirements.

12.3 Financial Controls

All financial transactions shall be properly authorized, documented and recorded. The Organization shall maintain appropriate internal financial controls.

12.4 Use of Funds

Organizational funds shall be used only for lawful activities that advance the objectives of GADEH CBO.

13 RECORDS AND ACCOUNTABILITY

The Organization shall maintain accurate and appropriate records, including:

  • Membership register
  • Minutes of meetings
  • Financial records
  • Asset register
  • Project records
  • Partnership agreements
  • Reports and official correspondence

Organizational records shall be maintained securely and accessed in accordance with applicable laws, policies and legitimate organizational needs.

14 PROGRAMMES AND PROJECTS

GADEH CBO may develop and implement programmes in areas including:

  • Agro-entrepreneurship
  • Climate-smart agriculture
  • Digital agriculture
  • Youth empowerment
  • Women empowerment
  • Water-efficient agriculture and irrigation
  • Environmental conservation
  • Tree planting and ecosystem restoration
  • Farmer training
  • Market access
  • Innovation and technology

15 PARTNERSHIPS AND RESOURCE MOBILIZATION

GADEH CBO may collaborate with government institutions, development partners, NGOs, private companies, financial institutions, educational institutions, farmer organizations, community groups and other lawful stakeholders.

The Organization may prepare proposals, seek grants, undertake fundraising activities and establish partnerships that support its objectives.

All partnerships shall be consistent with the mission, objectives, values and Constitution of the Organization.

16 CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Every office bearer and member shall disclose any personal, financial or other interest that may conflict with the interests of GADEH CBO.

A person with a conflict of interest shall declare the conflict and shall not improperly influence decisions concerning the matter.

17 DISCIPLINE

Members and office bearers shall maintain high standards of conduct and shall not engage in activities that damage the Organization or violate applicable laws.

Disciplinary measures may include:

  • Verbal or written warning
  • Written reprimand
  • Suspension
  • Removal from office
  • Termination of membership

No disciplinary action shall be taken without giving the affected person a reasonable opportunity to respond.

18 AMENDMENTS

This Constitution may be amended by the General Membership through a properly convened meeting and in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Proposed amendments shall be communicated to members before the meeting at which they are to be considered.

Constitutional Safeguard: No amendment shall be valid if it conflicts with applicable laws or regulatory requirements governing community-based organizations in Kenya.

19 DISSOLUTION

The Organization may be dissolved through a decision of the General Membership made in accordance with this Constitution and applicable laws.

Upon dissolution, all outstanding lawful obligations shall first be settled.

Any remaining assets shall not be distributed among members. They shall be transferred or applied in accordance with applicable law and the requirements of the relevant regulatory authority.

20 ADOPTION AND SIGNATURES

We, the undersigned founding members of Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO, hereby confirm that we have read, understood and adopted this Constitution as the governing document of the Organization.
