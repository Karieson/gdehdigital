Digital Innovation

Digital Skills • Technology • Innovation • Entrepreneurship • Community Transformation

Digital Innovation is a core program area of Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub (GADEH), designed to promote practical use of digital technologies, innovation and entrepreneurship for inclusive community development.

GADEH seeks to equip young people, entrepreneurs, farmers, community members and emerging enterprises with relevant digital knowledge and practical skills that can improve productivity, access to information, employment opportunities and participation in the digital economy.

1 Program Overview

Building Digital Capacity for Inclusive Growth

GADEH promotes digital innovation as a practical tool for solving local challenges and creating new opportunities. The program focuses on accessible, responsible and community-oriented technology rather than technology for its own sake.

Activities are designed to help participants understand, use and apply digital tools in education, agriculture, business, employment, communication, environmental conservation and community development.

2 Key Digital Innovation Areas
Digital Literacy Basic computer, smartphone, internet, online communication, information search and digital productivity skills.
Computer Skills Practical training in word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, file management, online services and workplace digital tools.
Digital Entrepreneurship Supporting participants to use digital platforms, online tools and technology to start, promote and grow small enterprises.
Digital Marketing Building practical skills in social media, digital branding, content creation, customer engagement and online promotion.
Technology for Agriculture Encouraging farmers and agribusinesses to use digital information, mobile tools and technology for better production and market access.
Online Learning Promoting access to digital learning resources, online courses, educational platforms and self-directed skills development.
Innovation & Problem Solving Encouraging young innovators to identify community challenges and develop practical technology-enabled solutions.
Digital Safety Promoting responsible internet use, privacy, cybersecurity awareness, secure accounts and protection of personal information.
3 Priority Participants
Youth Digital skills, innovation, employment readiness and technology entrepreneurship.
Entrepreneurs Digital business tools, marketing, online customer engagement and enterprise growth.
Farmers Digital agricultural information, market access and technology-enabled farming.
4 Digital Skills & Training

Computer Fundamentals

Introduction to computers, operating systems, keyboards, file management, internet use and essential workplace applications.

Office Productivity

Practical use of word processors, spreadsheets, presentations, email and digital document management.

Smartphone & Internet Skills

Effective use of smartphones, mobile applications, online search, digital communication and internet-based services.

Digital Content Creation

Introduction to creating useful digital content including graphics, presentations, social media materials and basic multimedia communication.

5 Technology for Agriculture & Agribusiness

Connecting Digital Innovation with Agriculture

GADEH encourages farmers and agribusiness entrepreneurs to use digital technologies to improve decision-making, access agricultural information and connect with markets.

  • Digital access to agricultural knowledge and extension information.
  • Mobile-based communication between farmers, buyers and service providers.
  • Digital marketing of agricultural products.
  • Use of online platforms to identify markets, opportunities and business information.
  • Digital record keeping for farms and small agribusiness enterprises.
6 Innovation & Digital Entrepreneurship

Idea Development

Participants are encouraged to identify real community problems and develop practical, sustainable and technology-supported ideas.

Digital Business

Training on using digital platforms to promote products, communicate with customers and create new business opportunities.

Youth Innovation

Supporting young people to explore technology, creativity and entrepreneurship as pathways to employment and community impact.

Local Solutions

Promoting affordable and locally relevant digital solutions addressing agriculture, education, enterprise and community challenges.

8 Community Impact

Digital Technology for Community Transformation

GADEH's digital innovation approach aims to ensure that technology contributes directly to community resilience, economic opportunity and inclusive development.

By combining digital literacy with agriculture, entrepreneurship, environmental awareness and youth empowerment, GADEH seeks to build a community that is better equipped to participate in the modern digital economy.

9 Partnership & Collaboration

Working Together for Digital Inclusion

GADEH welcomes collaboration with educational institutions, technology organizations, private-sector partners, development organizations, government institutions and other stakeholders interested in expanding digital opportunities within communities.

Potential areas of collaboration include digital skills training, innovation challenges, mentorship, technology access, entrepreneurship development, community digital centres and technology-enabled agricultural initiatives.
