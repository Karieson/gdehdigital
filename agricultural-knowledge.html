Learning Area 02

Water Management and Irrigation

Water is essential for crop establishment, growth and production. Understanding irrigation methods helps farmers make better use of available water resources.

Use water efficiently. Protect every drop.

Farmers can improve water management by selecting suitable irrigation systems, monitoring soil moisture, maintaining irrigation equipment and reducing unnecessary water losses.

The most appropriate irrigation method depends on crop requirements, soil characteristics, water availability, farm size and available resources.

Study Types of Irrigation →
  • Understand different irrigation systems.
  • Learn about drip and sprinkler irrigation.
  • Explore surface and furrow irrigation.
  • Monitor soil moisture and crop water needs.
  • Maintain pipes, pumps and irrigation equipment.
  • Practise water conservation.
Learning Area 03

Agricultural Pests and Crop Protection

Crop pests can damage leaves, stems, roots, flowers, fruits and seeds. Early identification and appropriate management help protect agricultural production.

1

Identify Pests

Learn to recognise common agricultural pests and distinguish harmful organisms from beneficial insects.

2

Inspect Crops

Regularly inspect leaves, stems, roots and fruits for visible pests and damage.

3

Prevent Damage

Apply appropriate crop management practices, field sanitation and preventive measures.

4

Manage Pests

Use suitable integrated pest management approaches and responsible crop protection methods.

Learn before applying pest treatments

Correct identification is important before selecting a control method. Avoid unnecessary pesticide application and follow approved product instructions whenever pesticides are required.
