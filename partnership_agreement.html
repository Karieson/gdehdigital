This Collaboration Agreement establishes the understanding between Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub (GADEH) and the collaborating organization, institution, business, agency, community group or other approved partner identified in this document.

The parties intend to work together in good faith to support agricultural development, digital empowerment, entrepreneurship, skills development, environmental conservation, youth empowerment, community development and other mutually agreed initiatives.

3 Purpose of Collaboration

3.1 Collaboration Objectives

The parties agree to explore and implement mutually beneficial activities that contribute to sustainable community development, agricultural innovation, digital empowerment and improved livelihood opportunities.

5 Responsibilities of the Parties

5.1 GADEH Responsibilities

  • Coordinate agreed activities in accordance with the approved collaboration plan.
  • Provide reasonable programme coordination and communication.
  • Facilitate community engagement where applicable.
  • Provide relevant programme information and updates.
  • Maintain appropriate records relating to agreed activities.
  • Promote responsible, transparent and accountable implementation.

5.2 Partner Responsibilities

  • Participate in agreed activities in good faith.
  • Provide agreed technical, financial, material or other support where applicable.
  • Designate an appropriate contact person for coordination.
  • Communicate relevant changes affecting implementation.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations and agreed programme requirements.

6 Financial and Resource Arrangements

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, this Collaboration Agreement does not by itself create an obligation for either party to provide funding or financial compensation to the other party.

7 Confidentiality and Data Protection

The parties shall take reasonable steps to protect confidential information exchanged in connection with the collaboration.

Personal information shall be collected, used, stored and shared responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements and the parties' agreed procedures.

Neither party shall disclose confidential information to an unauthorized third party except where disclosure is authorized, required by law or reasonably necessary for implementation of the collaboration.

8 Intellectual Property and Branding

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, trademarks, logos, documents, materials and other proprietary resources.

Use of either party's name, logo, photographs, publications, training materials or other protected content for public communication shall be subject to appropriate authorization.

9 Communication and Coordination

10 Duration and Termination

This Agreement shall commence on the agreed effective date and shall remain in effect for the period stated below unless extended, replaced or terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

Either party may request termination or modification of the collaboration by providing reasonable written notice to the other party, subject to any specific obligations already agreed for ongoing activities.

11 Dispute Resolution

The parties shall seek to resolve any disagreement arising from this Agreement through good-faith consultation and constructive communication.

Where an issue cannot be resolved through direct consultation, the parties may agree to use mediation or another appropriate dispute resolution mechanism before pursuing further legal remedies, subject to applicable law.

12 General Conditions

  • This Agreement represents the parties' current understanding concerning the stated collaboration.
  • Any significant amendment should be made in writing and acknowledged by both parties.
  • Neither party shall represent itself as the legal agent, employee or representative of the other party unless expressly authorized.
  • Collaboration activities shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations of the Republic of Kenya.
  • This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture or employment relationship unless expressly stated in a separate written agreement.
Declaration and Agreement

The undersigned representatives confirm that they are authorized to represent their respective organizations and that they have reviewed the terms of this Collaboration Agreement. The parties agree to cooperate in good faith and to undertake the agreed activities in a responsible, transparent and mutually respectful manner.
