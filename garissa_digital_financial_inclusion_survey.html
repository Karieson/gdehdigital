DIGITAL FINANCIAL INCLUSION & OPEN PAYMENTS SURVEY
Evidence, Challenges and Opportunities Among Youth in Northern Kenya

Purpose of the Survey

This questionnaire is designed to collect primary evidence on access to digital financial services, mobile money, digital payments, payment interoperability, financial literacy, online security, and opportunities for improving financial inclusion among young people and communities in Garissa County.

The information collected will be used for research and will be presented in aggregated or anonymized form. Participation is voluntary. You may decline to answer any question or withdraw from the survey.

Instructions to respondent: Please answer the questions honestly based on your own experience. Tick the appropriate box or write your response in the space provided. Do not write your name, national ID number, phone number, PIN, password, or other unnecessary personal identifying information.
