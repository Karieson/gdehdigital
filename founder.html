Professional Summary

Professional profile and areas of experience

Abdullahi Sheikh Aden is a dynamic and results-driven Information Technology professional with a Bachelor of Science in Information Science at undergraduate level and a Diploma in Information Technology.

He has experience in digital literacy training, ICT capacity building, technology implementation, community empowerment and practical digital solutions. His professional development includes training and certifications associated with YALI Network, Afralti, Microsoft Azure, Webb's Institute and St. John Ambulance.

In addition to his technology background, he brings more than 10 years of practical experience in ambulance and transport services, including emergency response, patient transport, first aid, defensive driving and long-distance navigation.

This multidisciplinary experience combines technical capability with operational discipline, safety awareness, community service, leadership, problem-solving and practical project implementation.
