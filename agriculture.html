Agriculture & Agribusiness

Empowering farmers, entrepreneurs and communities through sustainable agriculture, climate-smart production, digital innovation and inclusive agribusiness development.

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub (GADEH) promotes agriculture as a pathway to improved livelihoods, food security, employment creation and inclusive local economic development.

Our Agriculture & Agribusiness programme connects practical farming knowledge with entrepreneurship, digital tools, environmental stewardship and market-oriented agricultural production.

1 Programme Overview

Sustainable Agriculture

Promote productive farming systems that conserve soil, water and natural resources while improving household and community livelihoods.

Agribusiness Development

Support farmers and emerging entrepreneurs to treat agriculture as a viable business through planning, value addition, market access and enterprise development.

Digital Agriculture

Use digital technologies and information services to improve access to agricultural knowledge, markets, financial information and business opportunities.

Farmer Empowerment

Equip farmers, youth and community groups with practical skills, knowledge and networks that strengthen agricultural productivity and resilience.

2 Our Agriculture Approach

From Production to Sustainable Enterprise

GADEH takes an integrated approach that goes beyond agricultural production. Farmers and participants are supported to understand the complete agricultural value chain, from planning and production to harvesting, storage, processing, marketing and reinvestment.

  • Promote efficient and responsible use of land and water.
  • Encourage diversified and climate-resilient farming.
  • Strengthen farm management and record keeping.
  • Encourage value addition and post-harvest management.
  • Connect producers with markets and business opportunities.
  • Encourage youth participation in modern agriculture.
3 Priority Areas

Crop Production

Improved crop planning, sustainable production practices, soil management, irrigation and post-harvest handling.

Water-Efficient Farming

Promote responsible water use, efficient irrigation, water harvesting and technologies suitable for water-stressed communities.

Livestock & Mixed Farming

Encourage integrated agricultural enterprises that strengthen household resilience and diversify sources of income.

Agribusiness

Build entrepreneurial skills in agricultural enterprises, including budgeting, pricing, marketing and business planning.

Value Addition

Encourage processing, packaging, branding and other approaches that increase the value and marketability of agricultural products.

Agricultural Markets

Strengthen market awareness, buyer linkages, collective marketing and access to relevant agricultural market information.

4 Agricultural Value Chain
Planning Needs assessment, enterprise selection, budgeting and production planning.
Production Climate-smart farming, irrigation, crop and livestock management.
Value Addition Processing, grading, packaging, storage and quality improvement.
Markets Marketing, buyer connections, pricing and enterprise growth.
5 Climate-Smart Agriculture

Building Resilient Agricultural Communities

GADEH promotes agricultural practices that help farmers respond to changing environmental conditions while improving productivity and protecting natural resources.

  • Water-efficient irrigation and water conservation.
  • Soil conservation and soil fertility management.
  • Crop diversification and resilient farming systems.
  • Agroforestry and responsible tree planting.
  • Integrated pest and disease management.
  • Sustainable use of agricultural inputs.
  • Climate and weather information for farm decisions.
6 Digital Agriculture & Innovation

Connecting Agriculture with Digital Opportunity

Digital tools can help farmers and agricultural entrepreneurs make better decisions, access information and connect with markets.

  • Digital agricultural advisory and information sharing.
  • Digital record keeping and farm management.
  • Market and price information.
  • Digital marketing for agricultural products.
  • Online learning and agricultural skills development.
  • Digital entrepreneurship for youth in agriculture.

8 Agribusiness & Enterprise Development

Business Skills

Participants can develop practical knowledge in business planning, costing, pricing, budgeting, record keeping, customer service, marketing and enterprise management.

Market-Oriented Production

Farmers and entrepreneurs are encouraged to identify market opportunities before investing in production and to consider quality, consistency, demand and profitability.

Youth Agripreneurship

GADEH encourages young people to explore agriculture as an innovative business sector through technology, value addition, services and modern production systems.

Enterprise Linkages

Partnerships and networks can help agricultural enterprises access knowledge, markets, services, finance opportunities and technical support.

10 Agriculture & Environmental Conservation

Farming While Protecting Natural Resources

Sustainable agriculture depends on healthy soils, responsible water management, biodiversity and responsible land use. GADEH integrates environmental conservation into agricultural development.

  • Tree planting and agroforestry initiatives.
  • Soil and water conservation.
  • Responsible management of agricultural waste.
  • Protection and restoration of degraded land.
  • Promotion of environmentally responsible agricultural practices.
11 Expected Outcomes
  1. Increased knowledge and adoption of sustainable agricultural practices.
  2. Improved water-use efficiency in agricultural production.
  3. Stronger agricultural entrepreneurship and business management skills.
  4. Increased participation of youth and women in agricultural enterprises.
  5. Improved awareness of agricultural markets and value addition opportunities.
  6. Greater use of digital tools for agricultural information and enterprise development.
  7. Stronger community resilience and diversified livelihood opportunities.
  8. Increased awareness of environmental conservation within agricultural communities.
