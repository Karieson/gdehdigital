Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub CBO is a community-based organization dedicated to empowering youth through digital literacy and bridging the digital divide within Garissa County. The organization focuses on equipping young people, women, and persons with disabilities with essential digital skills needed in today’s technology-driven world. By creating opportunities for learning, innovation, and access to digital tools, the hub plays a key role in building a more inclusive and digitally skilled community in Northern Kenya.

Why It Was Formed

The organization was formed to address the growing gap in digital knowledge and access to technology among youth in Garissa and the wider Northern Kenya region. Many young people lack the skills required to compete in the modern job market, and this initiative aims to change that by providing affordable and accessible training. It also seeks to reduce unemployment, promote innovation, and empower individuals to use digital tools for education, entrepreneurship, and personal development.

When It Was Formed

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub CBO was established in early 2026 as a response to the urgent need for digital empowerment in the region. Since its formation, it has been committed to delivering impactful programs that support community growth and digital transformation.

Who Formed It

The organization was founded by ten dedicated youth members who strongly believe in gender equality and inclusive development. These founders came together with a shared vision of transforming their community by providing equal opportunities for all individuals, regardless of gender or background, to access digital education and skills.

Where It Operates

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub operates primarily within Garissa Township. However, its programs and opportunities are open to youth, women, and persons with disabilities across the wider Northern Kenya region. The hub aims to expand its reach and impact by welcoming participants from different areas who are eager to improve their digital skills.

How It Operates

The hub operates from Monday to Friday between 8:00 AM and 4:00 PM, offering structured training sessions that include both theoretical and practical lessons. In addition, it conducts workshop training sessions every Saturday of the month, following a six-month program structure. These workshops are designed to provide intensive hands-on experience and real-world application of digital skills. Upon successful completion of any program or workshop, participants are awarded a certificate of completion.

Eligibility

Any individual with a passion for digital literacy is eligible to join Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub either as a member or a student. The organization encourages participation from youth, women, and persons with disabilities, ensuring inclusivity and equal opportunity for all who wish to learn and grow in the digital space.

Cost of Training & Membership

The hub offers affordable access to digital training. For students, training is provided free of charge, with only a small maintenance fee of Ksh 500 required for registration and Ksh 700 for certificate processing upon completion. Membership registration costs Ksh 2,000, with an annual renewal fee of Ksh 3,000. For those interested in workshop training only, a fee of Ksh 1,000 is applicable. These fees are structured to ensure sustainability while keeping opportunities accessible to the community.
