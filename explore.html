About Us

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub CBO is a community-based organization dedicated to empowering youth through digital literacy and bridging the digital divide within Garissa County. The organization focuses on equipping young people, women, and persons with disabilities with essential digital skills needed in today’s technology-driven world.

The Hub creates opportunities for learning, innovation, entrepreneurship, and access to digital tools that support education, employment, and community development across Northern Kenya.

Why It Was Formed

The organization was established to address the growing gap in digital skills and access to technology among youth and vulnerable groups in Garissa and Northern Kenya.

Its mission is to empower communities through affordable and accessible digital training, promote innovation, reduce unemployment, and create opportunities for sustainable development.

When It Was Formed

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub CBO was established in early 2026 to support digital transformation and youth empowerment within the region.

Who Formed It

The organization was founded by ten dedicated youth members who strongly believe in inclusivity, equal opportunity, and community empowerment through technology and education.

Where It Operates

The Hub primarily operates within Garissa Township while welcoming participants from different areas across Northern Kenya who are interested in gaining digital and entrepreneurial skills.

How It Operates

The Hub operates Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM through structured learning sessions, practical workshops, mentorship, and digital training activities.

Weekend workshops are also conducted to provide additional hands-on learning experiences and specialized short courses.

Eligibility

Youth, women, persons with disabilities, and vulnerable community members with interest in digital literacy and entrepreneurship are eligible to participate in the Hub’s programs and training activities.

Cost of Training & Membership

Training programmes are designed to remain affordable and accessible to the community.

Students are only required to pay minimal maintenance and certification processing fees. Membership registration and workshop participation fees support sustainability of the programmes and operational activities.
