What is Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub?

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub (GDEH) is a Community-Based Organization (CBO) dedicated to empowering youth and the wider community through digital skills, innovation, and access to modern technology.

Why was it formed?

GDEH was formed to bridge the digital gap in Garissa by equipping individuals with essential skills needed in today’s digital world, improving livelihoods, and creating new opportunities.

When was it formed?

GDEH was officially established in the year 2026.

Reasons for the formation of GDEH CBO

The organization was formed to address unemployment, lack of digital skills, limited access to technology, and to promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and self-reliance among youth and communities.

Who formed GDEH?

GDEH was formed by 10 dedicated members committed to community development and empowerment. (See the Meet Our Team page for full details.)

Courses offered at GDEH

1: Smartphone Literacy

Learn smartphone usage, internet access, and digital safety. Builds confidence in mobile technology use.

2:Computer Literacy

Basic computer skills including typing, Microsoft Office, and internet use for education and jobs.

3: Agro-Entrepreneurship Literacy

Combines farming with business skills to create income-generating agricultural ventures.

4: Short ICT Courses

Includes coding, digital marketing, and graphic design for modern online job opportunities.
