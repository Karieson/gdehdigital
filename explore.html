About Us

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO is a community-based organization committed to empowering youth, women, farmers and vulnerable communities through digital skills, agro-entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture, innovation and community development.

The Hub combines technology and agriculture to create practical opportunities for learning, entrepreneurship, employment, innovation and sustainable livelihoods across Garissa County and Northern Kenya.

Why It Was Formed

The organization was established in response to the challenges facing young people and communities, including limited access to digital skills, employment opportunities, entrepreneurship support and modern agricultural knowledge.

Its purpose is to bridge the digital and agricultural opportunity gap by equipping communities with practical digital, entrepreneurial and climate-smart farming skills that can contribute to sustainable livelihoods and local economic development.

When It Was Formed

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO was established in early 2026 as a youth-led community initiative focused on digital empowerment, agro-entrepreneurship, innovation and sustainable community development.

Who Formed It

The organization was founded by a group of dedicated young community members who share a commitment to empowering communities through technology, agriculture, entrepreneurship and practical education.

The founding team believes that combining digital innovation with agriculture can create new opportunities for young people, farmers and entrepreneurs in Garissa and Northern Kenya.

Where It Operates

The Hub primarily operates within Garissa County while engaging with communities and participants from different parts of Northern Kenya.

Its activities may include digital training, agricultural capacity building, agro-entrepreneurship programmes, community outreach, mentorship and innovation activities.

How It Operates

The Hub operates through structured training sessions, practical workshops, mentorship, community outreach, entrepreneurship programmes and hands-on learning.

Its programmes bring together digital technology and agriculture to help participants develop practical skills that can be applied in business, farming, employment and community initiatives.

Training activities may include digital literacy, computer skills, digital entrepreneurship, agro-entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture, digital agricultural advisory, innovation and environmental conservation.

Eligibility

Youth, women, farmers, persons with disabilities, entrepreneurs, students and other interested community members are eligible to participate in the Hub's programmes, training activities and community initiatives.

Specific programme requirements may vary depending on the nature and objectives of each training or project.

Cost of Training & Membership

The Hub aims to keep its programmes affordable and accessible to the community while maintaining the quality and sustainability of its activities.

Depending on the programme, participants may be required to pay modest training, membership, materials or certification-related fees.

Where funding or partnerships are available, selected community programmes may be offered at subsidized or no cost to eligible participants.
