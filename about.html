About Us

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO (GADEH CBO) is a youth-led community-based organization established in 2026 to promote digital empowerment, agro-entrepreneurship, innovation and sustainable community development in Garissa and Northern Kenya.

We believe that technology, agriculture and entrepreneurship can work together to create practical opportunities for young people, farmers, women, persons with disabilities and other underserved members of the community.

Our programmes focus on digital literacy, computer and smartphone skills, ICT training, agro-entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture, agricultural innovation, entrepreneurship, mentorship and community capacity building.

Through practical training, partnerships and community engagement, we aim to equip individuals with knowledge and skills that can support employment, self-reliance, enterprise development and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision

To build digitally empowered and economically resilient communities in Garissa and Northern Kenya where young people, farmers and entrepreneurs have the knowledge, skills and opportunities to thrive through technology, agriculture and innovation.

Mission

Our mission is to provide inclusive, practical and accessible digital, agricultural and entrepreneurial training that enables communities to adopt technology, develop sustainable enterprises, improve agricultural productivity and create meaningful economic opportunities.

Core Values
  • Inclusivity – promoting equal access to opportunities, knowledge and technology.
  • Innovation – encouraging creativity, technology adoption and practical problem-solving.
  • Empowerment – equipping individuals and communities with skills that promote independence and self-reliance.
  • Sustainability – promoting solutions that create lasting social, economic and environmental impact.
  • Collaboration – working with communities, institutions, private sector organizations and development partners.
  • Integrity – maintaining honesty, transparency, accountability and responsible management.
Strategic Aims
  • Bridge the digital skills gap in Garissa and Northern Kenya.
  • Promote digital literacy and responsible use of technology.
  • Equip youth and women with practical agro-entrepreneurship skills.
  • Promote climate-smart and sustainable agricultural practices.
  • Support farmers in adopting appropriate digital technologies and agricultural innovations.
  • Promote youth entrepreneurship, employment and self-reliance.
  • Encourage innovation and technology-driven agricultural enterprises.
  • Create opportunities for mentorship, training, networking and community development.
  • Develop partnerships that expand access to knowledge, technology, markets and opportunities.
Our Areas of Focus
  • Digital Empowerment & Literacy – promoting practical digital skills including computer literacy, computer packages, internet use, email, smartphone literacy, digital communication and responsible use of technology.
  • ICT Skills & Short Courses – providing practical short courses in ICT, digital productivity, data entry, digital services, help-desk support and other relevant technology skills.
  • Graphic Design & Digital Creativity – equipping youth and community members with practical skills in graphic design, digital content creation, visual communication, branding and related digital creative tools.
  • Computer Repair & Technical Support – developing practical skills in computer maintenance, troubleshooting, basic hardware and software support, installation and technical assistance.
  • Smartphone & Mobile Technology – helping community members use smartphones, mobile applications, online services and digital communication tools effectively, safely and productively.
  • Agro-Entrepreneurship – supporting youth, farmers and emerging entrepreneurs to develop agricultural enterprises, value-addition activities, market opportunities and sustainable agribusinesses.
  • Climate-Smart Agriculture – promoting sustainable, productive and climate-resilient agricultural practices suited to local conditions.
  • Digital Agriculture – encouraging the use of digital technologies, information and tools to improve agricultural productivity, decision-making, market access and farm management.
  • Entrepreneurship & Innovation – supporting business development, creativity, problem solving, innovation and technology-driven enterprises.
  • Youth & Women Empowerment – creating opportunities for practical skills development, mentorship, entrepreneurship, innovation and meaningful participation in community development.
  • Environmental Conservation – promoting tree planting, ecosystem restoration, responsible natural-resource management and sustainable community practices.
  • Community Capacity Building – strengthening the knowledge, skills, confidence and participation of individuals and community groups through training, awareness and practical programmes.
Our Approach

We believe in a practical and community-driven approach to development. We listen to community needs, understand local challenges, test practical solutions, learn from experience and continuously improve our programmes.

Our approach can be summarized as:

Listen → Understand → Test → Learn → Improve → Scale

Who We Serve

Our programmes are designed to serve youth, women, farmers, entrepreneurs, students, persons with disabilities and other community members seeking practical skills and opportunities.

We particularly seek to reach underserved communities and individuals who face barriers to accessing digital technology, entrepreneurship opportunities and modern agricultural knowledge.
