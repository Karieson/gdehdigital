About Us

Garissa Digital Empowerment Hub CBO (GDEH CBO) was established in 2026 by a group of passionate youth determined to address the digital gap in Northern Kenya. We are driven by the belief that technology is not a privilege but a necessity for education, employment, and community growth.

Our programs include computer literacy, smartphone training, agro-entrepreneurship, ICT short courses, and mentorship initiatives. We provide affordable training designed to empower marginalized groups and foster innovation across Garissa County and beyond.

Vision

To transform Garissa and Northern Kenya into digitally empowered communities where every individual has the skills, confidence, and opportunity to thrive in the digital age.

Mission

Our mission is to deliver inclusive, practical, and affordable digital training that empowers youth, women, and persons with disabilities to unlock opportunities in education, employment, and entrepreneurship.

Core Values

• Inclusivity – ensuring equal access for all.
• Innovation – fostering creativity and problem-solving.
• Sustainability – building long-term community impact.
• Collaboration – working with partners to expand reach.
• Integrity – maintaining transparency and accountability.

Aims

• Bridge the digital divide in Garissa County.
• Empower vulnerable groups with ICT and entrepreneurial skills.
• Support youth employment and self-reliance.
• Promote digital literacy as a tool for social and economic development.
• Create a hub for innovation, mentorship, and community transformation.
