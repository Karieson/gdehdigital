1. About Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO (GADEH CBO) is a community-based organization committed to promoting inclusive socio-economic development through digital literacy, technology innovation, Internet of Things (IoT), entrepreneurship and agro-entrepreneurship.

The organization seeks to equip young people, women, farmers, entrepreneurs, students, job seekers and underserved communities with practical skills, technology knowledge and opportunities that can improve livelihoods and strengthen local economic development.

GADEH CBO recognizes technology and agriculture as important drivers of employment, entrepreneurship, food security, innovation and sustainable development.

Through training, mentorship, innovation, community engagement, partnerships and enterprise development, GADEH CBO seeks to bridge the digital divide and create practical pathways toward learning, employment, entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision

A digitally empowered and economically resilient community where technology, innovation and agro-entrepreneurship create sustainable opportunities for all.

Mission

To empower communities through digital literacy, Internet of Things, technology innovation, entrepreneurship and sustainable agricultural enterprise development.
