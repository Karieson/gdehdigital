1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Introduction

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO is committed to protecting the privacy, dignity and personal information of participants, members, trainers, partners, staff, visitors and users of our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and manage personal information provided through our website, registration forms, training programmes, membership activities, agro-entrepreneurship programmes, digital services and other organizational activities.

1.2 Information We Collect

Depending on the service or programme, we may collect information including:

  • Full name and contact information.
  • Phone number and email address.
  • Identification or registration information where required.
  • Age, gender and general demographic information.
  • Educational and professional background.
  • Training, membership and programme participation information.
  • Entrepreneurship, farming or agricultural programme information.
  • Documents submitted for programme or administrative purposes.
  • Feedback, enquiries and communication submitted to the Hub.
1.3 How We Use Personal Information

Information collected may be used for legitimate organizational purposes, including:

  • Programme registration and administration.
  • Training and learner management.
  • Membership management.
  • Communication with participants and members.
  • Certification and verification.
  • Monitoring and evaluation of programmes.
  • Community outreach and engagement.
  • Agro-entrepreneurship and agricultural programme implementation.
  • Reporting to authorized partners, funders or relevant authorities.
  • Improving our programmes, services and community initiatives.
1.4 Protection of Personal Information

We take reasonable technical, administrative and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Access to personal information is limited to individuals who require the information for legitimate organizational, programme or administrative purposes.

1.5 Sharing of Information

Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO does not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Information may only be shared where reasonably necessary with authorized programme partners, trainers, sponsors, funders, service providers, government authorities or other relevant organizations for legitimate programme, operational, reporting or legal purposes.

1.6 Programme Photography and Media

During training, community activities, agricultural demonstrations, workshops and events, photographs or videos may be taken for legitimate documentation, reporting, awareness and promotional purposes.

Where appropriate, participants may be informed about photography or media activities and may be provided with an opportunity to raise concerns regarding the use of their image.

1.7 Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil programme, administrative, reporting, certification, legal or accountability requirements.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps may be taken to securely delete, destroy or anonymize it.

1.8 Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and legitimate organizational requirements, individuals may request to:

  • Access personal information held about them.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where appropriate.
  • Raise concerns regarding the handling of their information.
  • Request clarification about how their information is used.
1.9 Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use basic cookies, browser technologies or analytics tools to improve website functionality, security, performance and user experience.

Users may manage or disable cookies through their browser settings, although some website features may not function properly as a result.

1.10 External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, platforms or services. Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of external platforms before submitting personal information.

1.11 Data Security Incidents

Where a personal data security incident occurs, the organization will take reasonable steps to assess the incident, secure affected information and respond in accordance with applicable requirements and organizational procedures.

1.12 Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be reviewed and updated periodically to reflect changes in our programmes, services, organizational practices or applicable legal requirements.
