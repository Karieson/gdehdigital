1 Purpose and Scope

1.1 Purpose

This document establishes the principles, procedures and conditions governing the collection and use of personal information and the use of organizational websites, programmes, services, facilities and digital resources.

It is intended to promote responsible information management, transparency, accountability, respectful participation and the protection of individuals who interact with the organization.

1.2 Scope

This Policy applies, where relevant, to members, programme participants, farmers, trainees, trainers, volunteers, staff, partners, beneficiaries, applicants, website visitors and other persons interacting with the organization.

2 Definitions

Term Meaning
Organization Refers to Garissa Agro-Digital Empowerment Hub CBO and its authorized programmes, representatives and activities.
Personal Data Information relating to an identifiable or identified individual.
Participant Any person registered for, attending or benefiting from an organizational programme or activity.
Website Any official website, online form, digital portal or electronic platform operated on behalf of the organization.
Programme Any training, agricultural, digital, entrepreneurship, environmental, youth or community initiative conducted or supported by the organization.

3 Privacy and Data Protection

3.1 Commitment to Privacy

The organization is committed to respecting the privacy, dignity and lawful rights of individuals whose personal information is collected or processed in connection with its activities.

3.2 Lawful and Responsible Processing

Personal information shall be collected and processed for legitimate organizational purposes and, where required, on an appropriate lawful basis or with appropriate consent.

Data Protection Principle:

The organization seeks to collect only information that is reasonably relevant to the purpose for which it is required and to handle such information responsibly.

4 Information We Collect

4.1 General Information

  • Full name and preferred name.
  • Telephone number and email address.
  • General location or county information.
  • Age or age category where relevant.
  • Gender or demographic information where required for programme monitoring.
  • Educational and professional background.

4.2 Programme Information

  • Training registration and attendance information.
  • Membership and participation records.
  • Programme applications and eligibility information.
  • Assessment, certification and completion records.
  • Feedback, complaints and enquiries.

4.3 Agricultural and Entrepreneurship Information

  • Farming activities and agricultural interests.
  • Crop and production information where relevant to a programme.
  • Entrepreneurship interests and business information.
  • Training needs and development priorities.
  • Programme monitoring and impact information.

4.4 Documents and Supporting Information

Where reasonably required for a programme or administrative process, participants may be asked to provide supporting documents or other information. Such information shall be handled according to applicable requirements and organizational procedures.

5 Use of Personal Information

Personal information may be used for legitimate organizational purposes, including:

  • Programme registration and administration.
  • Training and learner management.
  • Membership administration.
  • Communication with participants and members.
  • Certification and verification.
  • Monitoring, evaluation and programme learning.
  • Community outreach and engagement.
  • Agricultural and agro-entrepreneurship programme implementation.
  • Digital agricultural advisory and educational activities.
  • Reporting to authorized funders, partners or authorities.
  • Improving services and organizational programmes.
  • Maintaining appropriate organizational records.
Non-commercial principle:

The organization does not sell, rent or trade personal information as a commercial commodity.

6 Data Sharing and Disclosure

6.1 Authorized Sharing

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with authorized programme partners, trainers, sponsors, funders, professional service providers, government authorities or other relevant entities.

6.2 Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, lawful authority, regulatory requirements or legitimate legal processes.

6.3 Third-Party Services

Some digital, communication, payment, hosting or other services may be operated by third parties. Where such services process information on behalf of the organization, reasonable steps should be taken to use appropriate providers and safeguards.

7 Data Security and Retention

7.1 Security Measures

Reasonable technical, administrative and organizational safeguards may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

7.2 Access Control

Access to personal information should be limited to persons who require it for legitimate programme, organizational, administrative or legal purposes.

7.3 Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for programme administration, reporting, accountability, certification, legal or legitimate organizational requirements.

7.4 Disposal

When information is no longer required, reasonable measures may be taken to securely delete, destroy, anonymize or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements.

8 Rights of Data Subjects

Subject to applicable law and legitimate organizational requirements, individuals may request to:

  • Access personal information held about them.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where legally and operationally appropriate.
  • Raise concerns about the handling of their information.
  • Request clarification about how information is used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and withdrawal is legally applicable.
Important:

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, accountability, safeguarding, financial, certification or other legitimate organizational purposes.

9 Website and Digital Services

9.1 Website Use

Users are expected to use the website and digital services responsibly, lawfully and respectfully.

9.2 Cookies

The website may use basic cookies, browser technologies or analytics tools to improve functionality, security, performance and user experience.

9.3 External Websites

The organization's website may contain links to external websites, platforms or services. The organization is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of independently operated third-party websites.

9.4 Website Security

Users must not attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, databases, files, administrative areas or other restricted resources.

10 Photography, Media and Communications

10.1 Documentation

During training sessions, agricultural demonstrations, community activities, workshops, meetings and events, photographs or videos may be taken for legitimate documentation, reporting, awareness and programme communication purposes.

10.2 Responsible Use

The organization should seek to use photographs, videos and participant stories responsibly and in a manner consistent with the dignity and legitimate expectations of participants.

11 Terms and Conditions

11.1 Acceptance

By accessing the website, registering for a programme, becoming a member, attending training or using organizational services, a user agrees to comply with these Terms and Conditions.

11.2 Acceptable Use

The organization's facilities, website, digital resources, equipment and services shall be used only for lawful, educational, agricultural, entrepreneurial, community or otherwise authorized purposes.

11.3 Prohibited Activities

  • Unauthorized system access.
  • Fraud or deliberate misrepresentation.
  • Harassment or intimidation.
  • Damage or misuse of organizational property.
  • Unauthorized use of organizational accounts.
  • Distribution of unlawful or harmful content.
  • Threatening or disruptive conduct.
  • Any other unlawful activity.

12 Programme Participation

12.1 Registration

Registration or application does not automatically guarantee admission, selection or participation in a programme.

Admission may depend on eligibility, available spaces, programme requirements, funding, organizational priorities and other relevant considerations.

12.2 Attendance

Participants are expected to attend scheduled sessions and engage responsibly in training, workshops, practical activities, agricultural demonstrations and other programme activities.

12.3 Assessments and Certificates

Certificates may be issued only to participants who satisfy the applicable attendance, participation, assessment and completion requirements.

A certificate may be withheld or withdrawn where it was obtained through fraud, false information or serious misconduct.

13 Code of Conduct

All participants, members, staff, volunteers, trainers and other persons participating in organizational activities are expected to maintain respectful and professional conduct.

  • Respect the dignity and rights of others.
  • Do not engage in harassment, bullying or intimidation.
  • Do not engage in discriminatory or abusive conduct.
  • Do not commit fraud, impersonation or deliberate misrepresentation.
  • Protect organizational property and equipment.
  • Respect programme rules and instructions.
  • Protect confidential information.
  • Use digital systems responsibly.
  • Report serious concerns through appropriate channels.

Violation of the Code of Conduct may result in warning, suspension, termination of participation, withdrawal of access or other appropriate action.

14 Intellectual Property

14.1 Organizational Materials

Unless otherwise stated, organizational logos, branding, training materials, publications, documents, graphics, videos, photographs, website content and other original materials are owned by the organization or their respective rights holders.

14.2 Restrictions

Materials shall not be reproduced, distributed, modified, published or commercially exploited without appropriate authorization where authorization is required.

14.3 Participant Contributions

Where participants contribute photographs, stories, ideas, testimonials or other materials for programme documentation, appropriate permissions and applicable rights shall be considered before such materials are used publicly.

15 Agricultural and Entrepreneurship Information

15.1 Educational Purpose

Agricultural, agro-entrepreneurship, business, digital and entrepreneurship information provided through organizational programmes is intended primarily for educational and informational purposes.

15.2 Individual Decisions

Participants remain responsible for evaluating information and making their own farming, business, financial or investment decisions.

Agriculture and enterprise notice:

The organization does not guarantee specific agricultural yields, business profits, investment returns, employment outcomes or other financial results from participation in its programmes.

16 Payments, Fees and Refunds

16.1 Programme Fees

Where a programme requires training, membership, materials, certification or other fees, the applicable charges and payment requirements shall be communicated to participants before payment where reasonably practicable.

16.2 Refunds

Refund and cancellation arrangements may vary depending on the specific programme, service, funding arrangement or applicable terms communicated at the time of registration.

16.3 Payment Records

Reasonable payment and transaction records may be maintained for accountability, financial administration, auditing and reporting purposes.

17 Complaints and Dispute Resolution

17.1 Complaints

Participants and other stakeholders may raise reasonable complaints, concerns or enquiries relating to organizational services, programmes, privacy, conduct or information handling.

17.2 Good-Faith Resolution

The organization will seek, where reasonably practicable, to address legitimate complaints fairly, respectfully and in good faith through appropriate internal procedures.

17.3 Safeguarding Concerns

Concerns involving serious misconduct, abuse, threats, exploitation or other safeguarding matters should be reported through the appropriate organizational reporting channels or relevant authorities where necessary.

18 Amendments and Governing Law

18.1 Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions may be reviewed and updated periodically to reflect changes in programmes, organizational practices, technology or applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

18.2 Effective Date

An updated version shall take effect when formally adopted or published through an appropriate organizational communication channel, unless a different effective date is stated.

18.3 Governing Law

These Terms and Conditions shall be interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations of the Republic of Kenya.
