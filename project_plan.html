4. Key Project Risks and Mitigation

Risk Potential Effect Mitigation Measure
Limited internet connectivity Disruption of digital training Use multiple connectivity options and maintain backup connectivity.
Low participant retention Reduced completion rates Flexible schedules, mentorship and participant follow-up.
Equipment damage or loss Reduced training capacity Asset management, security controls and maintenance.
Limited employment opportunities Reduced economic outcomes Develop employer, freelancer and entrepreneurship partnerships.
Insufficient sustainability after funding Reduced continuation of activities Develop revenue-generating services and strategic partnerships.
Implementation principle: The project will prioritize practical learning, inclusion, measurable outcomes, youth ownership, sustainability and responsible use of technology.
