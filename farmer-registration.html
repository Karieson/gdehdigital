Building a Connected Farmer Community

The GADEH Digital Farmer Portal aims to make agricultural knowledge, digital learning and community engagement more accessible to farmers.

About Farmer Registration

Farmer registration is intended to help GADEH understand the agricultural interests and learning needs of participating farmers.

Through the portal, farmers can express interest in agricultural training, digital literacy, crop production, livestock management, water conservation and other relevant learning opportunities.

Registration is intended to support communication and participation in future farmer-focused activities. It does not automatically guarantee access to funding, grants, inputs, employment or other benefits.

Why Participate?

  • Access agricultural learning resources.
  • Develop digital skills for farming.
  • Identify relevant learning opportunities.
  • Share agricultural challenges and experiences.
  • Participate in community knowledge-sharing.
  • Express interest in future GADEH activities.
